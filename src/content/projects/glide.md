Glide shows the pictures already on your PC: no accounts, no ads, no internet.

Open it and every picture is there, newest first and grouped by month, without
any folders to click through. The same goes for your other drives, a USB stick,
and an Android phone plugged in by cable. Memories brings back what you took on
this day in years past.

- **Every picture, by month:** dated by when it was taken, not when it was copied.
  Photos and screenshots can be shown apart.
- **The formats cameras use:** JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, HEIC, AVIF, TIFF and RAW files
  from most cameras.
- **Edit a copy:** crop, rotate, light and colour adjustments, and filters. You can
  also blur or replace the background behind the subject, found on your PC.
- **Make something new:** cut the subject out as a sticker, put a few pictures
  together in a collage, or compare two side by side, then and now.
- **Tidy up:** rename, copy, move, or send pictures to the Recycle Bin, one at a time
  or many at once.
